Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Do I need an appointment?

Not for hand washes — just drive in. For full details, ceramic coatings, and larger jobs we recommend booking ahead so we can reserve the time and give you an exact quote.

Is it really 100% by hand?

Yes. We never run your vehicle through automated brushes. Every wash is done by hand and towel-dried, which is far gentler on your paint and gives a better finish.

How long does a wash or detail take?

An express hand wash is usually about 20 minutes. "The Works" runs a bit longer, an express detail about 45 minutes, and full details with ceramic coating are typically scheduled for a few hours or same-day pickup.

How much does detailing cost?

Interior details start at $149, exterior details at $179, and full detail with ceramic coating from $299. Final pricing depends on your vehicle's size and condition — call (516) 220-3000 or request a quote for an exact figure.

How does the Unlimited Wash membership work?

For $29/month you get unlimited "The Works" hand washes at either location, a priority lane, free tire shine, and 10% off detailing. There's no contract and you can cancel anytime. Just ask our team to set it up on your visit.

Do you handle trucks, SUVs and vans?

Absolutely. Larger vehicles are welcome — pricing is a little higher than a standard sedan depending on size. We also service business fleets and dealership inventory.
